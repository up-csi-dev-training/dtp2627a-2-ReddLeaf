Facts and BasicAnalysis about the Song:
- It is the  Japanese city pop debut single by Miki Matsubara in 1979 before turning 20 years old
- The song title literally means "Midnight's Door" and its main theme shows nostalgia and longing about a previous relationship.
- The title refers to a physical door in a house which is sang in the chorus where she knocks the door and/or door to someone's heart
